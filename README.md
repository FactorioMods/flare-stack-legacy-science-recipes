# Flare Stack: Legacy Science Recipes

Keep existing science-pack incineration assignments when upgrading an older factory to Factorio 2.1 with Flare Stack 4.3.1 or later.

Factorio 2.1 classifies science packs as items. Flare Stack therefore generates their incineration recipes under `item-` names instead of the former `tool-` names. This companion supplies native recipe-name migrations for the twelve base and Space Age science packs. Existing incinerators keep their assigned recipe and paid crafting progress.

Requires Factorio 2.1.24 or later and Flare Stack 4.3.1 or later. Space Age is optional; its recipe mappings are harmless when those packs are absent. Enable the companion when upgrading a factory that used the older science recipe names. It adds no recipes, machines, runtime scripts or changes to incineration balance.
